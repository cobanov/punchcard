What it does

The commits come with the hours.

Every tracker can tell you that Tuesday had six hours in it. punchcard tells you which commits happened inside them, in which repositories, on which branches — including the branch you were actually working on, which is the part most integrations quietly miss.

When you forget

A timer you forgot to start is not lost work.

The commits are already there and already timestamped. punchcard shows them as punches with no card around them, and turns them into a record when you say so. That is a better answer than guessing at idle time, because the evidence is real.
